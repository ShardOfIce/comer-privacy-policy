Privacy Policy

Application: Talk Fluent (package com.shardofice.comer)
Provider: Aleksandr Kazanov pr Šiljakovac, Šiljakovac, Serbia (“Talk Fluent”, “we”, “us”).
Last updated: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Talk Fluent (the “App”) collects, how we use it, who processes it for us, how long we keep it, and how you can delete it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Account. When you first open the App, an anonymous account is created for you automatically (Firebase Authentication) and identified by a random account ID. If you choose to sign in with Google, Google provides your email address, name and profile photo to Firebase Authentication; the App itself only uses your email address (to show which account you are signed in with and to pre-fill the reply address of a support request).
  • Messages and voice. The text you type in chats, and your voice during a voice call (recorded only while a call is active, after you allow microphone access), are sent to our servers to generate replies, corrections, explanations, translations and transcriptions. They are processed in real time and are not stored on our servers. Your chats and saved words are stored on your device.
  • Learning preferences. Your native language, the language you practise and your level. Interests, age and gender you may enter stay on your device.
  • Coins, rewards and purchases. Your coin balance, daily-reward streak, the number of rewarded ads watched per day, and records of verified Google Play purchases (product and purchase token). We never receive your payment-card details.
  • Device identifier. Your device’s Android ID, which our server immediately converts into a one-way hash. It lets us restore your coins after you clear the App’s data or reinstall it, and prevents welcome coins from being claimed repeatedly.
  • Usage statistics. Counts only: number of messages, saved words and chats, which companions you chat with, your languages and the time of your last use, linked to your account ID. We also use Firebase Analytics, which records events such as completing onboarding, sending a message or starting a call, together with an app-instance identifier and basic device information.
  • Advertising. If you choose to watch a rewarded ad to earn coins, Google AdMob may collect your device’s advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about your interaction with the ad. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before ads are personalised.
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports and basic device information (Firebase Crashlytics), so we can fix problems.
  • Support requests. The text of your request, the email address you give us (optional), your account ID and the App version. Feedback you leave in the introductory tour contains only the text and your languages.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the App: AI conversation partners, corrections and explanations, translation, voice calls, word games and your vocabulary.
  • To keep your account, coin balance, rewards and purchases, to verify purchases with Google Play, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To show rewarded ads you choose to watch.
  • To understand how the App is used, fix crashes and improve the App.
  • To answer your support requests.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the performance of our contract with you (providing the App), our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, improving the App) and, for personalised advertising, your consent.

3. Who processes your information

We do not sell your personal data. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • OpenAI (United States) — receives your chat messages, voice audio during calls and the words you look up, through our backend, to generate replies and transcriptions. See openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
  • Google — Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database in the EU (europe-west1), Cloud Functions in the US (us-central1), Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check), Google Cloud Translation, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob. See policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy. When you watch a rewarded ad, AdMob may use the data described above for advertising under Google’s own policies.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

4. International transfers

Our database is in the European Union, but messages, voice and translations are processed in the United States (our Cloud Functions and OpenAI). These providers protect such transfers with safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep your information

  • Chats and saved words: on your device, until you delete them or the App’s data.
  • Account, coins, rewards, purchase records, usage statistics and support requests: until you delete your account.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.
  • After you delete your account we keep only data that no longer identifies you: one-way hashes of credited purchase tokens and of your device ID with a “deleted” mark, so purchases can’t be credited twice and welcome coins aren’t granted again. Google Play keeps its own records of your orders.

6. Deleting your account

In the App, open Profile → Delete account and confirm. Your account and the data listed in section 5 are deleted immediately, and the App’s data on your device is erased. You can also request deletion without the App: see How to delete your account.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (including the EU/EEA under the GDPR and Serbia under the Law on Personal Data Protection), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to it, and to data portability. You can withdraw consent for personalised ads at any time. To exercise your rights, contact us at severshard@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, or under 16 in the EU/EEA. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below these ages. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). API credentials are kept on our backend, never in the App, and backend access is limited to the genuine App (Firebase App Check). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at severshard@gmail.com.
