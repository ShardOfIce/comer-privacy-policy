This Privacy Policy explains how Talk Fluent (the “App”) accesses, collects, uses, and shares your information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

  • Content you provide. Text messages you type and voice recordings you make while chatting with the AI companions, plus the vocabulary words you save.
  • Learning preferences. Your native language, the language you are practicing, and your proficiency level.
  • Usage and analytics data. A randomly generated installation identifier, timestamps, the number of messages and saved words, the languages you use, and snapshots of your chat history. This is used to understand product usage and improve the App.
  • Diagnostics. Crash reports and basic device/diagnostic information collected to keep the App stable.
  • Purchase information. If you buy a subscription or in-app product, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive confirmation of the purchase but never your full payment-card details.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate AI responses to your messages and voice input.
  • To provide text-to-speech playback and translation of messages.
  • To detect the language of your messages (performed on your device).
  • To save your progress, vocabulary, and preferences.
  • To measure usage, fix crashes, and improve the App.
  • To process and validate purchases.

3. How your information is shared

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers that make the App work:

  • OpenAI — your text and voice messages are sent (through our secure backend) to OpenAI’s API to generate responses. See OpenAI’s privacy policy at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy.
  • Google / Firebase — we use Google Cloud (Text-to-Speech, Translation) and Firebase (Realtime Database, Crashlytics) to deliver features, store analytics, and report crashes. Firebase data is stored in the European Union (europe-west1). See policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Google Play Billing — processes subscriptions and purchases.
  • Legal reasons — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

4. Data storage and retention

Preferences, vocabulary, and chat history are stored on your device and, for analytics, in Firebase (EU). We keep analytics data only as long as needed for the purposes above. You can clear local data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App.

5. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location (including the EU/EEA under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To exercise these rights, or to request deletion of your data, contact us at severshard@gmail.com.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, or under 16 for users in the EU/EEA. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below these ages. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including keeping API credentials on our backend rather than in the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at severshard@gmail.com.
