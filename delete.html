Delete your Talk Fluent account

Application: Talk Fluent by Cassandra Studio (package com.shardofice.comer)
Last updated: 7 November 2026

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Talk Fluent and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete.

Your account is deleted right away, and the App’s data on your phone is erased. The next time you open the App it starts from scratch.

Without the app

If you signed in with Google, email severshard@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Talk Fluent account” from the Google address you signed in with (or tell us that address). We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

A guest account (never signed in with Google) can’t be identified by email, so please delete it in the app as described above.

What is deleted

  • Your account (anonymous and Google sign-in) and its coin balance, including purchased coins (they are not refunded).
  • Daily-reward and ad-reward history, and records of your purchases.
  • Usage statistics linked to your account and your support requests.
  • When deleted in the app: your chats, saved words and settings on the phone.

What is kept

  • One-way hashes of credited purchase tokens and of your device ID with a “deleted” mark, without any account ID — so purchases can’t be credited twice and welcome coins aren’t granted again on the same device. They are kept as long as the App is offered.
  • Google Play keeps its own records of your orders.
